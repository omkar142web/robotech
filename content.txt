# About

### Where ideas become machines.

RoboTech is a student-led community built around curiosity, engineering, and hands-on learning.

We explore robotics, electronics, embedded systems, programming, automation, and emerging technologies by actually building things.

From simple sensor-based robots to autonomous systems, members learn by designing, testing, breaking, improving, and building again.

**No experience required.** Start with the basics, contribute to ongoing projects, or take on something ambitious.

### Our Mission

**Learn by building. Build by experimenting. Grow by solving real problems.**

We create a space where students develop technical skills, creativity, leadership, and problem-solving through robotics.

# How We Build

### From idea to working machine.

Every project follows a simple cycle:

**01 — Ideate**
Find a problem. Explore possible solutions.

**02 — Design**
Plan the hardware, mechanics, electronics, and software.

**03 — Build**
Turn the design into a working prototype.

**04 — Program**
Develop the logic and control systems.

**05 — Test**
Put it through real-world conditions. Find what breaks.

**06 — Improve**
Fix, refine, repeat.
