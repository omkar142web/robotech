01 About

Where ideas become machines.

A student community built around curiosity, engineering and hands-on learning. No experience required.

RoboTech explores robotics, electronics, embedded systems, programming and automation by actually building things — from simple sensor robots to autonomous systems. We design, test, break, improve, and build again.

01

Robotics

Mobile robots, kinematics and autonomous behaviour.

02

Programming

Embedded logic, control systems and real code.

03

Electronics

Sensors, microcontrollers, motors and circuits.

04

Innovation

Emerging tech turned into working prototypes.

“Learn by building. Build by experimenting. Grow by solving real problems.”

— OUR MISSION
